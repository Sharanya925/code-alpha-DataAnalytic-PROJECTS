# Data story: what drives book prices?

- **Scope:** 1,000 books scraped from Books to Scrape.
- **Typical price:** median £35.98, middle 50% between £22.11 and £47.46.
- **Rating vs price:** Spearman correlation 0.03 (little to no relationship), so a higher star rating does not by itself mean a higher price.
- **Categories:** highest average price is Travel (£39.79), lowest is Food and Drink (£31.41).

**Decision takeaway:**  Travel is the priciest of the larger categories (£39.79) and Food and Drink the cheapest (£31.41), but the gap is small and not statistically significant (category test p = 0.952). Price also does not depend on star rating, so a store should not assume that highly rated or specific-category books deserve higher prices. Pricing decisions should look at other factors, such as stock levels or book content.
